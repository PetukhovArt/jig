New names come from the nearest glossary: atom `domain-model`, the `feature` and `module-design` flows send the agent to it, and a delegate brief names the glossary line each new term comes from. `jig review rules` finds a module's own glossary on the walk up from the changed files and prints an `avoid` line for each added name a glossary's `_Avoid_` line lists; every review judges these as naming candidates, with or without a conventions angle (#34).
