jig-review posts the deepening proposal as its own resolvable thread (chip `**[💡 Deepening]**`) instead of a section of the FYI note: `review route` prints it as a `discussion` until `deepening.discussion_id` is written, `review state` prints `deepening yes|posted|no`, a re-review answers a developer reply there like one under a Nit, and an open deepening thread never makes a merge request stale, returned or held (`review-cycle unresolved` skips it by its chip). An FYI note left with no section is deleted, so a note an earlier run wrote with only the deepening goes away once the deepening posts as its thread.
