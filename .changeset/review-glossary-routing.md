`jig review rules` and `jig review-plan` route the project's glossaries: the root `GLOSSARY.md` to every finder, a context glossary that `GLOSSARY-MAP.md` lists to the partitions under its directory, printed in each finder's `rules:` line. A name in the diff that a glossary lists to avoid (`_Avoid_`, `Avoid:`, `не использовать`) is a naming finding with the glossary line quoted.
