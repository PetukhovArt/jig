jig-video records on macOS (avfoundation, System Events) and Linux X11 (x11grab, wmctrl) as well as on Windows: `fv.mjs check` reports this OS's requirements, `["window", "place" | "pin", ...]` scenario items replace the PowerShell calls, edge-tts resolves as a command or a python module, and the subtitle font comes from the OS.
