# 5 · Wrappers and proxies

**Class:** bug. **Category:** `correctness`. **Atom:** `system-edges`.

**Trigger:** a type that wraps another: cache, adapter, decorator, proxy.

**Two checks:**
1. **Routing.** Every method calls exactly the wrapped instance, not a fresh lookup through a registry or a global. Otherwise the call comes back into the wrapper itself: re-entry and recursion.
2. **Completeness.** Every method the calling code uses is exposed. A missing method fails at runtime.

**Evidence:** the line of the method that goes to the wrong place, or the call of a method the wrapper lacks.
