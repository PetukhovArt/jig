# prototype

**You own the design decision, not the code. The prototype is a throwaway instrument. The real build follows `flows/feature.md`.**

Atoms: design-it-twice, questions.

1. Scope the decision: the one question the prototype answers, and what result would pick each option.
2. Logic or state question: a small runnable script or terminal app in a scratch directory outside the source tree. Visual question: two or three variants behind one switcher in the running app.
3. Make the variants radically different, not three shades of one idea.
4. Observe each variant on the real surface: the output, the timing or the screen.
5. Recommend one option with the evidence, then hand the decision to `flows/feature.md`.
6. Delete the prototype or leave it outside the source tree, and say where.

Reply: the variants, the evidence for each (output, timing, screenshots), the trade-offs, your recommendation, the scratch path. Say plainly that the prototype is throwaway.
